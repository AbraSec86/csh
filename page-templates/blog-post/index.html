    # Specific, outcome-oriented title

Open with the useful conclusion or question. Give readers enough context to decide whether the article is relevant.

<!-- more -->

## Context

Explain the lab, research question, or learning objective without exposing sensitive or restricted information.

## What I observed

Separate verified observations from interpretations. Use sanitized examples and link to evidence only when it is safe and publishable.

## Why it matters

Connect the observation to a broader mental model, defensive implication, or assessment practice.

## Practical lessons

State what should change in a methodology, technique, control, or learning plan.
