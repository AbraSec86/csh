    Cybersecurity Handbook¶

    This handbook turns study notes and lab experience into a connected system for lawful, authorized security assessments. It separates durable concepts from repeatable procedures and case-specific writeups.

    Authorization is a prerequisite

    Use the operational material only in labs you control or on systems for which you have explicit written authorization. Rules of engagement, safety constraints, and applicable law always take precedence over a playbook.

    The organizing rule¶

    Section Primary question Expected lifetime
    Knowledge Why does this work? Long-lived
    Technique How do I test one hypothesis? Medium-lived; verify regularly
    Playbook When and in what order do I work? Medium-lived
    Writeup What happened in this specific case? Historical
    Blog What did I learn or conclude at this point in time? Time-oriented
    Field operations How do I work safely and professionally? Long-lived
    Reporting How do I communicate impact and remediation? Long-lived
